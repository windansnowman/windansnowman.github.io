excerpt: "Most existing LLM safety evaluation and defense methods follow a static formulation: jailbreak vulnerabilities are evaluated with fixed attack methods, and guardrails are trained on fixed malicious prompt datasets. However, real-world adversaries continuously evolve their attack capabilities and expand the attack space. To address this challenge, DARWIN formulates jailbreaking as an open-ended evolution process and continuously updates guardrails through an evolving attack-defense loop. DARWIN-Attack expands attack capabilities through strategy discovery, mutation, and selection, while DARWIN-Guard iteratively trains guardrails on emerging adversarial samples generated by DARWIN-Attack. Through continuous evolution, DARWIN-Attack achieves state-of-the-art attack success rates against frontier LLMs and guardrails, and DARWIN-Guard achieves an average unsafe recall of 91.6% across 12 safety evaluation benchmarks while maintaining an average pass rate of nearly 100% on standard benign datasets."
