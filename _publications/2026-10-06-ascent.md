excerpt: "ASCENT aims to improve LLM safety and downstream performance together during fine-tuning. It uses a first-order model of safety changes to derive a low-rank calibration update and a task update that limits harmful interference while retaining useful learning. Periodic recalibration refreshes the safety-related structure as the model changes. Experiments across several model families and downstream tasks report utility improvements of up to 20.3% and attack-success-rate reductions of up to 35.5%."
